# What changed from the April 2026 version, and why

## Errors in the old version

| Old claim | Problem | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Enrolled = students whose outcome is unknown at data collection | The dataset authors assign the label at the end of the normal course duration. Enrolled students are **delayed**, an observed state. | Reframed throughout; new title. |
| LR has the highest binary AUC (0.9516) | RF (0.9554) and GB (0.9547) were higher in the old Table 2 itself. | All numbers generated by code. |
| Binary to multiclass gap: F1 0.218, Acc 0.160, AUC 0.062 | Old tables give 0.222, 0.155, 0.082. "0.218 points (21.8%)" mixed points and percent. | Computed by `make_tables.py`. |
| Early prediction retains 87-92% | Old Table 4 gave 87.9-96.5%. "12.1 percentage points" was a relative %. | Recomputed. |
| Table 5 baseline accuracy 0.7063 | Equal to F1, a copy error; real accuracy about 0.766. | Table replaced. |
| SelectKBest k=20 keeps 99% of F1 | Selection was evaluated on the test split. Done inside CV, every reduced set is worse. | Nested selection. |
| GB beats LR in multiclass ("non-linear interactions") | Single split; with one-hot encoding of nominal codes LR is statistically tied. | Corrected tests. |
| SHAP waterfall values on standardised scale while "trees get raw input" | SHAP model was trained on scaled data. | XGBoost on raw features, and it is now in the main comparison, so no proxy. |
| Future work: SHAP | Already done in the paper. | Removed. |
| Sub-RQ 2 / Sub-RQ 3 | Not defined anywhere; left over from the proposal. | RQ1-RQ4 only. |

## Citation problems found

- Kemper et al. (2020): author names were wrong (real: Lorenz Kemper, Gerrit Vorhoff, Berthold U. Wigger), and the paper is an empirical study at KIT with logistic regression and decision trees, not a review of 42 studies. Three claims attributed to it were removed.
- Realinho et al. (2022): author first names were wrong (real: Valentim Realinho, Jorge Machado, Luis Baptista, Monica V. Martins).
- Chung and Lee (2019): real authors Jae Young Chung and Sunbok Lee; data are 165,715 Korean high-school students. The "85.4% accuracy" and the lifetime-earnings claim were not supported and were removed.
- Gu and Zhang (2026): real authors Hongwen Gu and Yuqi Zhang.
- Vo et al. (2026): real authors Nghia Trong Vo, Quang Nhat Le, Hang Le; full title and DOI added.
- Howard et al. (2018): first author is Emma Howard.
- Kohavi (1995) recommends stratified cross-validation; it was cited as support for bootstrap CIs.
- Efron and Tibshirani (1997) is about the .632+ error estimator, not percentile CIs. Removed.
- "UNESCO: OECD dropout 30-50%" had no source. Removed.
- Villar and de Andrade (2024): reported AUC is "above 0.90", not 0.93.
- Martins et al. (2023, Applied Sciences) could not be verified and was replaced by Martins et al. (2021, WorldCIST), which is verified on dblp.
- Removed as unverified or unused: cho2023, hamim2022, asselman2023, masood2024, putra2025, romero2025, fernandes2019, lee2019, molinaro2005, niyogisubizo2022, efron1983.

## Method changes

- 5-fold CV repeated 5 times instead of one 80/20 split; Nadeau-Bengio corrected t-tests.
- XGBoost and LightGBM added (8 models).
- One-hot encoding of the nine nominal integer codes for LR, SVM and MLP.
- New settings: enrolment-only, first semester, both semesters.
- Class-wise F1 and Dropout-vs-rest AUC reported.
- Calibration (reliability curves, ECE, Brier).
- Subgroup analysis: dropout recall and false-alert rate with bootstrap CIs, including age groups.

## Added in the second round

- Alert lists of fixed size (top 5-50% of the cohort): precision and reach at each prediction point.
- Group-specific thresholds (scholarship, age), cross-fitted, compared with one global threshold.
- Sensitivity analysis without the fee-status variables, whose recording time is undocumented (87% of students behind on fees drop out, so possible leakage).
- Nested hyperparameter tuning for LR, RF, XGBoost and LightGBM: no significant change.
- Appendix: full tables at enrolment and after the first semester, SHAP for the Enrolled class, hyperparameter table.

## Added in the third round

- Leave-one-programme-out validation (17 programmes): within-programme ranking transfers, probabilities need recalibration; logistic regression transfers better than XGBoost.
- Found a recording artefact: in Animation and Multimedia Design, 84% of students have no curricular units recorded in either semester (78% of its graduates).
- Bibliography: Villar (2024) first author is Alice Villar.
